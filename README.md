# Retail Sales & Refund Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
This interactive Power BI dashboard delivers comprehensive analysis of retail business performance, customer behavior, product sales, and refund trends. Built using historical retail transactional data from 2009–2011, the dashboard transforms raw data into actionable business insights through advanced DAX calculations and interactive visualizations.

## Key Insights

- Strong Q4 performance with peak sales recorded in November ($2.2M).
- United Kingdom generated the highest sales and refund activity.
- December recorded the highest monthly refund amount ($220K).
- Manual transactions contributed the largest refund value ($340K).
- Overall refund rate represents 6.24% of total gross sales.

---

## Business Recommendations

### Sales Optimization
- Increase marketing efforts during high-performing months such as Q4.
- Strengthen customer retention strategies in the United Kingdom market.
- Analyze seasonal sales fluctuations between November and December.

### Refund Reduction
- Further investigation is recommended for the refund spike in December.
- Review manual transactions with high refund values.
- Improve quality control for products with elevated refund activity.
- Implement refund monitoring alerts for abnormal refund increases.

### Performance Improvement
- Increase inventory levels for top-performing products.
- Set higher YoY growth targets for future sales periods.
- Continue monitoring customer purchasing behavior and refund trends.
