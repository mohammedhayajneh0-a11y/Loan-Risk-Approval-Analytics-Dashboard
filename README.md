# Power BI Portfolio Projects

## 2. Loan Risk & Approval Analytics Dashboard

### Overview
This interactive **Power BI** dashboard provides comprehensive analysis of loan portfolios, risk assessment, approval behavior, and borrower financial profiles. Built with real lending data, it helps financial institutions identify risk patterns, optimize approval processes, and make data-driven lending decisions using advanced DAX and interactive visualizations.

### Key KPIs
| KPI                          | Value          |
|-----------------------------|----------------|
| Total Loans                 | 32,000         |
| Total Loan Amount           | $310.88M       |
| Approval Rate               | 22%            |
| Average Interest Rate       | 11.01%         |
| Average Annual Income       | $65.89K        |
| High Risk Loan %            | 25.09%         |
| High Risk Approval Rate     | 44.65%         |
| High Risk Rejected Rate     | 55.35%         |

### Key Insights
- Overall approval rate is relatively low at **22%**, indicating strict lending criteria.
- **Low Risk** borrowers dominate the portfolio, while **High Risk** loans represent 25.09% of the total.
- High-risk applications face a significantly higher rejection rate (**55.35%**).
- Education and Venture loans have the highest number of rejections.
- Strong correlation exists between annual income and loan amount.
- Borrowers with previous defaults have notably lower approval rates.

### Business Recommendations

**1. Risk Management**
- Apply stricter scrutiny and enhanced due diligence for High Risk borrowers.
- Closely monitor loans with high burden ratios.
- Strengthen evaluation processes for applicants with previous default history.

**2. Loan Approval Optimization**
- Reassess approval criteria for **Education** and **Venture** loans due to high rejection volumes.
- Develop segmented approval strategies based on risk level and income.
- Leverage income-to-loan ratio insights for more balanced lending decisions.

**3. Portfolio Performance**
- Increase the proportion of Low Risk loans to improve overall portfolio quality.
- Optimize interest rate strategies according to risk profiles.
- Implement early warning systems for high-risk segments.
